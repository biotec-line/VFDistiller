## Executive Summary & Visibility Audit

VFDistiller (Variant Fusion Distiller) is a local-first bioinformatics desktop application for processing, converting, filtering, and annotating research-grade VCF, gVCF, 23andMe raw text, and FASTA variant data.

### Store Withdrawal & Open Science Positioning
On 2026-04-12, VFDistiller was withdrawn from the Microsoft Store listing to maintain a crisp "Research Use Only" boundary under IVDR (EU) 2017/746 without regulatory ambiguity. It is distributed exclusively via GitHub under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
