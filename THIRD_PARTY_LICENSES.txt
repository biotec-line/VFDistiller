## Zero-Copyleft Contagion for Genomic Research Data

All input sequencing files, user genomic datasets, filtered variant results,
annotated output VCFs, and generated reports (CSV, Excel, PDF) are scientific
research artifacts owned exclusively by the researcher or institution. They are
never subjected to AGPL-3.0-or-later copyleft contagion or licensing obligations.

## LGPL Dynamic Linking Compliance Statement

`pystray` is licensed under the LGPL-3.0-or-later and is linked dynamically via
unmodified PyPI wheel distributions in accordance with LGPLv3 Section 4. Users
retain full rights to inspect, update, or replace the `pystray` dependency in
their local Python environment. VFDistiller retains full functionality even if
`pystray` is omitted.
